NSEP Exam Pattern & Marking Blueprint

Conducted annually by the Indian Association of Physics Teachers (IAPT).

Part A1 (Single Correct) 60 Questions

• Marking: +3 for correct, -1 for wrong.

• Max Marks: 180 Marks

• Focus: Conceptual speed, dimensional analysis, standard standard results.

Part A2 (Multiple Correct) 10 Questions

• Marking: +4 if all correct are chosen, 0 otherwise.

• Max Marks: 40 Marks

• Penalty: No negative marks, but strict all-or-nothing.

Historical Topic-Wise Marks Distribution:

Domain Avg. Questions Weightage (%) Key Topics
Classical Mechanics 22 - 26 ~35% Rotation, Center of Mass, Gravitation, SHM, Fluid Dynamics
Electromagnetism 18 - 22 ~28% Gauss Law, Dielectrics, Ampere Law, EMI, AC, Poynting Vector
Optics & Wave Physics 10 - 13 ~16% Wave Optics (YDSE with thin films), Fermat's Principle, Doppler
Thermodynamics & KTG 8 - 10 ~12% Carnot Efficiency, Entropy change, Mean free path, Real gases
Modern Physics 6 - 8 ~9% Bohr Model, Photoelectric, de Broglie, Nuclear Decay, X-Rays

MI & MAS Criteria

MAS (Minimum Admissible Score): 50% of the average of the top 10 scores nationwide.

MI (Merit Index): 80% of the average of top 10 scores. Scoring above MI guarantees selection to INPhO (Stage 2).

Target Score for INPhO: 135 - 150 / 220

Recommended Books

  • 1. H.C. Verma: Concepts of Physics (Vol 1 & 2) - Core foundation.
  • 2. I.E. Irodov: Problems in General Physics - Mechanics & Electrodynamics.
  • 3. Halliday, Resnick, Krane: Vol 1 & 2 - Deep theoretical nuance.
  • 4. S.S. Krotov: Aptitude Test in Physics - Ideal for multi-concept Stage 1/2.
